# Background catalogue (the renderer picks one per clip)
Use with: --bg image:assets/backgrounds/<file> [--bg-trim N]
Mood/clip-type guide. Always view the clip's frames first; pick what contrasts with the clip's colours and fits its mood.

| file | look | best for | --bg-trim |
|---|---|---|---|
| background.png | warm sunset skyline, palms, pink-orange sky (792x252, soft) | default / chill / funny moments / daytime gameplay | 10 |
| city-night-ferris-wheel.png | night city, teal + purple neon, ferris wheel | night/dark gameplay, chases, tense or neon-coloured clips | 40 (Google size label baked in bottom-right) |
| key-art-collage.jpg | busy official-style key-art collage with logo, 2560x1440, sharp | hype/launch/reveal/"first time" moments; clips with dark or muted colours. Very busy: it is blurred behind the clip, sharp only in the bottom band | 10 |
| logo-crab-festival-beach.png | bright tropical beach bar scene with big VI logo | funny/laid-back/daytime reaction clips; avoid when the clip is already bright/green | 40 (label baked in) |
| tokyo-billboard-lucia-jason.png | PHOTO of a Tokyo billboard (street, shops) | AVOID - real-world photo, not game art; only use if the user asks | 40 |

NOTE (campaigns): key-art-collage.jpg and logo-crab-festival-beach.png show the GTA VI LOGO. Content Rewards campaigns for Lacy/Clix/YourRAGE/Kai/AsianJeff/Dylan ban logos - do not use these two for those creators.
